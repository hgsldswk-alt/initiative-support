Help

Add a name and initiative roll for each combatant, then start the encounter. Use Next turn to advance or Undo to restore the previous state. Table view shows a larger public order. Turn off Show on table for any name you want to keep private.

Open Rosters to reuse a group, paste a roster code, or export a local JSON backup. Codes carry public names and their order. A backup can also contain private combatants and HP. Keep it with people you trust.

Open Pro to see the local price or restore an earlier purchase. The 30-day trial begins at first launch. Core turn tracking and existing records remain available when the trial ends.

Privacy

The developer collects no data through the app. Encounters and rosters are stored on the device. The app uses no accounts, ads, analytics, or developer servers. Apple handles purchases and review requests. Files and images leave the app when you choose a sharing or export destination.

Widget names are hidden by default. If enabled, the widget shows public names subject to system lock screen privacy settings.

使用帮助

填写每位角色和实掷先攻，再开始战斗。用「下一回合」推进，「撤销」可恢复上一步。打开同桌看板可看大字顺序。编辑不想公开的角色，关闭「在同桌看板显示」。

在「名单」中复用角色、粘贴口令或导出本地 JSON 备份。口令只含公开角色名和顺序。备份可含私密角色和生命值，请交给你信任的人。

在「高级版」查看当地价格或恢复购买。30 天试用从首次打开起算，到期后基础功能和已有记录仍可用。

隐私

开发者不会通过本 App 收集数据。战斗和名单保存在设备上。应用无账号、广告、分析工具或开发者服务器。购买和评价由 Apple 处理。你选择分享或导出目的地后，相应文件或图片才离开本 App。

小组件默认隐藏角色名。打开显示后只提供公开角色名，并遵循锁屏隐私设置。
